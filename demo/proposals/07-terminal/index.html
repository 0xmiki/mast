Mast

A Chromium-based browser whose one job is blocking distractions. The blocklist lives in the browser itself, so there is no extension to disable.

Pick categories or sites, choose how long, press Block. Blocks can be made longer but never shorter, and forever means forever. The only way back is the 10-second Undo.

Attention

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Attention
illustration
The concentration of awareness on some phenomenon to the exclusion of other stimuli.

Attention is the cognitive process of selectively concentrating on one aspect of the environment while ignoring others. It has been described as the allocation of limited processing resources, and as a bottleneck that decides which information reaches working memory.

Researchers commonly distinguish selective attention, the ability to focus on a single source of information, from divided attention, the attempt to follow several at once. Performance on divided-attention tasks typically drops as the number of tasks rises.

Attention and technology

Notifications, infinite feeds and autoplay are designed to capture attention. Studies of task switching report that resuming a task after an interruption carries a measurable cost, which has led to tools that limit access to distracting websites for chosen periods.

Such tools differ in how easily a block can be undone. Some allow a block to be lifted at any time; others make blocks binding for their full duration, on the reasoning that a block which can be turned off in a weak moment offers little protection.
