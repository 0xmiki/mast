Proposal 08 · Brutal

Dark neo-brutalism as a system: one 2px stroke, one 4px hard shadow that collapses when pressed, square corners, flat fills. Ember is a slab that only ever means blocked.

08 · Brutal

The system

Principles
One stroke, one shadow.2px everywhere. A 4px hard shadow, never blurred, never another offset.
Raised means current.Only the active tab, the picked card, the primary action and floating layers rise.
Pressing is travel.The slab moves 4px into its shadow. The light never changes.
Inverted means this one.Checked, selected, highlighted and open all flip to off-white on black.
Ember is a block.Filled is forever, outlined is timed. Ember means nothing else.
